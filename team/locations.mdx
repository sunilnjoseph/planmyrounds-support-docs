A **location** represents a physical site your organisation runs from — for example, _Town Campus_ or _Ranipet Campus_. If your team operates out of a single site, you likely don't need locations at all; they're built for organisations that staff more than one.

<Note>
  Locations are independent of **department** and **pod** — a third, optional way to slice your team, alongside the two you may already use.
</Note>

## Add a location

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Locations">
    Go to **Settings** and select the **Locations** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new location">
    Select **Add Location**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Location Name** — for example, _Town Campus_ — and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create**. The location is immediately available to assign to doctors and to select on rosters, Shift Schedules, and Auto Duties.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only admins can add or edit locations.
</Note>

## Assign doctors to locations

Open a doctor's profile, select **Edit**, and check one or more locations under **Locations**. A doctor can belong to more than one — useful for staff who split time between sites. See [Doctor profiles](/team/doctor-profiles).

## What locations scope

| Feature | How location is used |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto Duty** | A genuine eligibility filter — only doctors assigned to the Auto Duty's location are included in its doctor pool. |
| **Rosters** | Display and filtering only — a doctor doesn't need to share the roster's location to be added manually. |
| **Shift Schedules** | Required when creating one — each Shift Schedule belongs to a single location. |
| **Dashboard and Duty Calendar** | Available as a filter, alongside department. |
