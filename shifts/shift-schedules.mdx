A **Shift Schedule** is a named, reusable weekly pattern built from your [Shift Requirements](/shifts/overview). Instead of picking individual shifts every time you build a roster, save the combination once and select it wherever you need it.

<Note>
  A Shift Schedule doesn't duplicate a shift's own times or staffing — it just references your existing Shift Requirements. If you edit a Shift Requirement later, every Shift Schedule that uses it picks up the change automatically.
</Note>

## Shift Requirements vs. Shift Schedules

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Shift Requirements" icon="clock" href="/shifts/overview">
    A single shift definition — name, start/end time, staffing, and recovery rules. The building block.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Shift Schedules" icon="layer-group">
    A weekly pattern of Shift Requirements — the same set every day, or a different lineup per weekday. What you pick when building a roster or Auto Duty.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Open Shift Schedules

Select **Shift Schedules** in the sidebar to see every saved schedule for your organisation.

## Create a Shift Schedule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new schedule">
    Select **Create New** (or open an existing one and **Edit**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Enter a **Name** (for example, _MICU Day + Night_) and an optional **Description**. Choose a **Location** (required) and, optionally, a **Department**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the pattern shape">
    Under **Schedule Pattern**, choose whether these shifts repeat every day or vary by weekday:

    - **All Days** — one list of shifts that applies to every day of the week.
    - **Weekdays** — a separate list of shifts for each day, so weekends or specific weekdays can differ from the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add shifts">
    Select **Add Shift** on the **All Days** card, or on each individual day card if you chose **Weekdays**. Pick from your existing Shift Requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy a day's shifts (optional)">
    On the **Weekdays** layout, once a day has shifts, use **Copy to All Days** or **Copy to Weekdays** to apply the same lineup elsewhere instead of re-adding shifts one by one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the schedule. It's immediately available in the roster wizard and the Auto Duty wizard's Shifts step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Active vs. inactive

Every Shift Schedule is **Active** by default. Deactivating one stops it from being offered for **new** rosters and new Auto Duty setups — it doesn't touch anything already built with it.

To deactivate, open the schedule and toggle it off. You'll be asked to confirm:

<Warning>
  Deactivating a Shift Schedule means it will no longer be selectable when creating a roster.
</Warning>

<Note>
  An inactive Shift Schedule still appears in the Shift Schedules list for editing or reactivating — it's only hidden from the picker inside the roster and Auto Duty wizards.
</Note>

## Where it's used

- The **roster creation wizard's** Shift configuration step can select a saved Shift Schedule instead of building the pattern from scratch each time.
- The [Auto Duty wizard's](/auto-duty-planning/setting-up-an-auto-duty) Shifts step requires picking one — it's what every planning cycle generates against.
