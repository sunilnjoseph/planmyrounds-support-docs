The **Dashboard** gives you a month-by-month read on how well-staffed your organisation is, without opening every roster individually. Use it to spot coverage gaps and workload imbalances before they become a problem.

## Open the dashboard

Select **Dashboard** in the sidebar. Use the month arrows to move between periods, and the **Department** and **Location** filters to narrow the view.

<Note>
  If there's no published roster for the selected month, the dashboard shows an empty state — generate a roster for that period to see its stats.
</Note>

## What you see

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Coverage" icon="shield-check">
    Required, covered, and gapped staffing slots for the month, plus the overall coverage percentage.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Workforce" icon="users">
    Total staff, how many are scheduled versus unscheduled, and how many sit within, above, or below their expected monthly hours.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Needs Attention" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    A flagged list of the month's most pressing issues, most severe first.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Upcoming · Next 7 Days" icon="calendar-clock">
    Uncovered shifts, understaffed shifts, and staff unavailability coming up in the next week.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Staff directory

A searchable table of every doctor in scope for the month, with their department, duties, scheduled hours, expected hours, and a status badge — **Within**, or flagged **Above** / **Below** their configured range.

## A doctor's own dashboard

Every doctor's profile has its own **Dashboard** tab — the first tab you see when you open a profile. It shows that doctor's duties, scheduled hours, expected range, status badge, night and weekend duty counts, and an **Upcoming** list of what's next, all for the selected month.

Use it to answer "how is this specific doctor doing this month?" without cross-referencing the org-wide view.
