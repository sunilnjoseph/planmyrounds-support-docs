An **Auto Duty** is a recurring definition that plans your roster for you, cycle after cycle — weekly or monthly — without you rebuilding it by hand each time. Set it up once, and PlanMyRounds notifies your team, opens a window for availability and leave, generates the schedule, and (if you choose) publishes it, all on its own.

<Note>
  Auto Duty is a separate, additional way to get a roster — it doesn't replace the [manual Duty Plan wizard](/rosters/creating-a-roster). Every cycle it runs still produces an ordinary **Duty Plan**, generated into the same rosters you'd otherwise build by hand.
</Note>

## The 5-stage planning cycle

Every cycle moves through the same stages, shown as a stepper on the cycle page:

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Notify** | The team gets a heads-up email/push, and the availability window opens. |
| **Availability & Leave** | Staff update their availability at any time; admins review leave in parallel — these run side by side, not one after the other. |
| **Generate** | The engine solves the roster against the current doctor pool, Shift Schedule, and rules. |
| **Roster Review** | The generated Duty Plan is ready to check — coverage, workload, and any soft violations — before it goes live. |
| **Published** | The Duty Plan is live and visible to the team. |

A cycle that can't produce a valid schedule lands in **Failed** instead of Roster Review; one you stop partway through lands in **Cancelled**. See [Running a planning cycle](/auto-duty-planning/running-a-planning-cycle) for what to do at each stage.

## How much runs on its own

You choose how hands-on each cycle is:

| Setting | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** (default) | You start each cycle and click **Generate Duty Plan** and **Publish Roster** yourself. |
| **Generate duty plan automatically** (Auto Mode) | The cycle generates on its own once the review window closes, even if some responses are still pending. You still review and publish. |
| **Publish automatically** | Requires Auto Mode. The generated Duty Plan publishes with no review step at all. |

See [Setting up an Auto Duty](/auto-duty-planning/setting-up-an-auto-duty) for how to configure these.

<Warning>
  The doctor pool is never a saved list — it's queried fresh at Notify and Generate time from your department, pod, and location filters. A doctor who's deactivated, transferred, or rotated out is automatically dropped from the next cycle; no manual list to maintain, but also nothing to manually pin down.
</Warning>

## Where to find it

Select **Auto Duty Planning** in the sidebar to see every Auto Duty in your organisation, its cadence, its Shift Schedule, and its latest planning cycle.
